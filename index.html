03 — GUARDRAILS

A person stays in charge.

AI should take work off your team's plate without taking decisions out of their hands. Every system we put in follows the same rules.

APPROVAL GATES

Sensitive steps wait for a yes

Routine work runs on its own. Anything that moves money, contacts a customer in a new way, or changes a record can be set to pause until someone on your team approves it.

VISIBILITY

Every action is logged

You can see what the system did, when, and why, and hand any case back to a person at any point.

ACCESS

We never need your passwords

Audits run on view-only walk-throughs of your tools. When we build, access is scoped to the job and stays in your accounts.
