GrowthOS data-deletion instructions

Version: 20 October 2026

Send a request

Email info@vibeworksstudio.ai with the subject “GrowthOS data deletion.” Identify the relevant business, Meta account/Page or information and a reply address. Do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary applicant information.

Responsible administrator and timing

Ivan Jackson handles requests manually. Our targets are acknowledgment within 7 days and completion or a substantive update within 30 days. Where more work is needed, the update will explain outstanding steps and any identified limitations.

Verification and review

We ask only for information needed to verify your authority and locate the relevant records. We review VibeWorks-held working records and applicable connected-service records. The review can include Grok shared files and conversations, Meta records, and client systems. Cloudflare D1 or GHL applicant records are included where they exist within the request’s scope; their presence is not assumed.

Deletion and confirmation

We remove information within our control where appropriate and coordinate required action with the relevant provider or client administrator. We report completed actions and identify any information retained, reasons, recovery or backup limits, and outstanding provider steps. Deleting a Grok bot alone may leave shared files or sessions. Provider-held records may require separate action and may remain subject to recovery windows or retention requirements.

Disconnecting GrowthOS

You can separately remove GrowthOS authorization through the relevant Meta account settings. Disconnecting future access and deleting previously retained information are different actions. Contact us if you need help identifying the applicable records or administrator.
