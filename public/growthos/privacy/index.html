Information we use

This website uses GitHub Pages and Cloudflare; GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

We do not represent these unverified flows as active, or guarantee that no applicant data exists in connected client systems. We review relevant systems when handling a request and will update this notice to describe confirmed additional processing.

Retention and deletion

Retention varies across working records, connected services, logs and backups. We do not currently apply one verified automatic deletion schedule across those systems. Recording a future retention date does not itself delete a record. Requests are handled manually by Ivan Jackson. We target acknowledgment within 7 days and completion or a substantive update within 30 days. A status update may identify provider action, verification or other work still required; it is not a guarantee that all provider copies are erased within 30 days.

Your choices and provider limits

We verify authority and act on records within our control, coordinating with providers or client administrators where needed. We explain any retained records, reasons and unresolved provider or backup limits identified during review. Removing app authorization does not delete previously retained records. Provider recovery windows, security records and applicable retention requirements can limit immediate erasure. See our data-deletion instructions for the process.
